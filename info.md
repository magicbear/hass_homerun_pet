# 霍曼宠物 (Homerunpet) Home Assistant Custom Integration

[![HACS Custom](https://img.shields.io/badge/HACS-Custom-orange.svg)](https://hacs.xyz/)
[![Home Assistant](https://img.shields.io/badge/Home%20Assistant-2024.1+-blue.svg)](https://www.home-assistant.io/)

Control and monitor your Homerunpet (霍曼宠物) smart pet devices directly inside Home Assistant!

## ✨ Highlights / 核心特性
- 🔐 **Zero Cloud Sniffing**: Native Config Flow in HA UI — simply log in with your phone number or email.
- 🐈 **Cat Litter Box (智能猫砂盆)**: Monitor litter percentage, waste bin full alert, cat occupancy/proximity, and trigger remote cleaning & sand leveling.
- 🍲 **Smart Feeder (智能喂食器)**: Monitor food remaining, battery level, outlet block alerts, set portions, and trigger instant feeding.
- 💨 **Air Purifier / Deodorizer (智能净味器/净化器)**: Control fan speeds, mode presets (Auto/Sleep/Strong), and track real-time PM2.5 & TVOC & filter life.
- 📦 **Pet Dryer (智能宠物烘干箱)**: Monitor temperature and humidity, change dryer modes, set countdown timer, and control lamp.
- 💧 **Smart Water Fountain & Heater**: Monitor water quality, fountain status, and heater temperature.
- 🌏 **Multi-Region Cloud**: Seamless support for China Mainland (`+86`), North America, and Europe servers.
